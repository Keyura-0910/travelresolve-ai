# Policy Document: Booking Confirmation & Vouchers
Document ID: POL-CONF-003
Category: Booking Confirmation

## 1. Confirmation Delivery
- Automated booking confirmation emails containing the 7-character booking reference (e.g., TRV1025) and detailed hotel voucher are dispatched within 5 minutes of successful payment.
- If the customer does not receive their confirmation, agents should first verify that the customer's email address matches the database file and suggest checking spam/junk filters.

## 2. Re-issuing Confirmation Records
- Support agents can immediately re-send digital vouchers to the verified email address on record.
- Inquiries asking to verify whether a reservation is active and secured must confirm:
  - Exact property name and location
  - Check-in and check-out dates
  - Booked room type and number of guests
  - Current booking status (must be "confirmed")

## 3. Pending Confirmations
- If a booking status is "pending", the partner hotel reservation system has up to 30 minutes to generate the property confirmation number. Customers should be advised that their room is reserved while the property acknowledges receipt.
