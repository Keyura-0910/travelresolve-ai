# Policy Document: Payment Troubleshooting & Resolution
Document ID: POL-PAY-005
Category: Payment Issue

## 1. Handling Failed Transactions
- When a payment status is "failed", reservations are temporarily held for 24 hours to allow the customer to provide an updated payment method.
- Customers must be directed to use the secure self-service payment update link in their account portal. Support agents and AI systems must NEVER ask for or record raw credit card numbers or CVV codes in chat or ticket logs.

## 2. Duplicate or Disputed Charges
- If a customer reports a duplicate charge, verify whether the transactions represent actual settled charges or temporary bank authorization holds.
- Pre-authorization holds are automatically released by the card-issuing bank within 3 to 7 business days.
- If two identical settled charges exist for a single booking ID, the finance team must be notified to void or refund the duplicate within 24 hours.

## 3. Currency and Billing Discrepancies
- Rates are quoted in the selected currency, but foreign transaction fees may be levied by the customer's card issuer. Explain that bank conversion rates are set by the card network.
