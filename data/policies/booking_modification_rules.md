# Policy Document: Booking Modification Rules
Document ID: POL-MOD-004
Category: Booking Modification

## 1. Permitted Modifications
- Date changes, room type upgrades, and guest name adjustments are subject to hotel room availability and rate differences.
- Date adjustments on flexible rates do not incur an administrative fee if requested more than 72 hours prior to check-in. The customer must pay any difference between the original rate and current daily rates.
- Shortening a stay less than 48 hours before check-in is treated as a late cancellation for the removed nights.

## 2. Guest Information Updates
- Minor corrections to spelling of guest names or updating special requests (e.g., quiet room, high floor, feather-free pillows) can be performed anytime without fee.
- Changing the primary guest name to an entirely different individual requires verifying customer identity to prevent unauthorized ticket transfers.

## 3. Non-Modifiable Bookings
- Non-refundable rate reservations cannot be rescheduled or modified to different dates without supervisor override.
