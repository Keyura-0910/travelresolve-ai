# Policy Document: Hotel Check-In & Property Guidelines
Document ID: POL-CHK-006
Category: General Travel Query

## 1. Standard Check-In & Check-Out Times
- Standard check-in time across partner properties is 3:00 PM local time. Standard check-out time is 11:00 AM local time.
- Properties with 24-hour reception desks accommodate late check-ins after 11:00 PM, provided the reservation is confirmed. Guests arriving late should have a late check-in notification recorded on their booking file.

## 2. Early Check-In and Luggage Storage
- Early check-in before 3:00 PM is subject to room availability upon arrival and cannot be guaranteed in advance unless an extra night is booked.
- Almost all partner hotels provide complimentary, secure luggage storage prior to check-in and following check-out.

## 3. Required Identification and Incidentals
- The primary registered guest must be at least 18 years old (21 at select casino or US resort properties) and present a valid government-issued photo ID (passport or driver's license).
- A physical credit card matching the primary guest's name is required at check-in for an incidental security deposit (typically $50 - $100 per night).
