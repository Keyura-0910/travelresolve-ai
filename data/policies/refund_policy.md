# Policy Document: Refund Processing Policy
Document ID: POL-REF-002
Category: Refund

## 1. Refund Eligibility and Calculation
- Refunds are issued only for eligible bookings in accordance with the specific rate terms purchased.
- Full refunds apply when a flexible reservation is cancelled at least 48 hours before check-in.
- If a booking was already cancelled and the payment status is marked "paid", the calculated refund amount will automatically be scheduled for transmission.
- Taxes and mandatory resort fees prepaid to our platform will be refunded on a pro-rata basis if the room rate is refunded.

## 2. Refund Processing Timeframes
- Once approved, refunds are transmitted to the merchant payment gateway within 1 to 2 business days.
- Depending on the customer's financial institution and card issuer, the funds typically appear on the customer's credit or debit card statement within 5 to 10 business days.
- For international transactions or PayPal payouts, processing may require up to 14 business days.

## 3. Disputed Charges and Manual Overrides
- Support representatives and automated AI agents are strictly prohibited from directly authorizing cash refunds exceeding $500.00 without Tier-2 supervisory sign-off.
- Any manual refund adjustment requires an explicit audit log record and human approval.
