# Policy Document: Cancellation Policy
Document ID: POL-CANC-001
Category: Cancellation

## 1. Standard Cancellation Windows
- Reservations booked under the Standard Flexible Rate can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours prior to local hotel check-in time (typically 3:00 PM local time on check-in day).
- Cancellations made between 24 and 48 hours prior to check-in will incur a cancellation fee equal to 50% of the first night's room charge plus applicable local taxes.
- Cancellations made less than 24 hours prior to check-in or failure to show up ("No-Show") will forfeit the entire cost of the first night of the booking.

## 2. Non-Refundable Rates
- Bookings explicitly labeled as "Non-Refundable" or promotional flash deals cannot be cancelled or modified for a cash refund once confirmed.
- In extraordinary medical or compassionate emergencies (with formal documentation), support agents may NOT unilaterally approve a refund, but MUST escalate the case to a Support Supervisor for review of a one-time travel credit or voucher.

## 3. Cancellation Procedure
- Customers can initiate cancellations via the self-service web portal or by requesting support assistance.
- Support agents must verify the booking ID, customer name, and check-in timeline in the system before confirming cancellation eligibility.
